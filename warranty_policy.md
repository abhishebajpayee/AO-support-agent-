# Warranty Policy

All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage,
water damage, or unauthorized repairs.

To claim warranty service, you need your order ID and a description of the
issue. Approved claims are either repaired, replaced, or refunded at our
discretion, typically within 10-15 business days.

Warranty is void if the product's seal has been tampered with or if it was
purchased from an unauthorized reseller.
