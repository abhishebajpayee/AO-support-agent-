# Return & Refund Policy

Items can be returned within 15 days of delivery if unused and in original
packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive
the returned item.

Refund amount depends on payment method:
- Prepaid orders: full refund to original payment method
- Cash on Delivery orders: refund issued as store credit, or bank transfer if
  requested

Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or refunded.

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or
wrong item being shipped, in which case a full refund including shipping is
issued.
