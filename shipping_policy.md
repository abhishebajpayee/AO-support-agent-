# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within India. Express shipping
takes 2-3 business days and costs an additional 149 INR.

Orders are shipped from our Pune warehouse. Once shipped, you will receive a
tracking link via email and SMS.

We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not supported.

If your order has not arrived within 10 business days of the estimated
delivery date, contact support and reference the "Delayed Shipment" policy
for expedited resolution.
